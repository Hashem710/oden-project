Lasagna Recipe

  • Home Page

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 pound ground beef or sausage (optional)
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil or parsley for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. If using ground beef or sausage, cook it in a skillet over medium heat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese, egg, salt, and pepper.
  5. Spread a thin layer of marinara sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.
  6. Layer 4 lasagna noodles, half of the ricotta mixture, half of the meat (if using), and one-third of the mozzarella cheese.
  7. Repeat the layers, ending with noodles and marinara sauce on top. Sprinkle remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  8. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes or until cheese is bubbly and golden.
  9. Let it cool for 10 minutes before serving. Garnish with fresh basil or parsley if desired.
  10. Enjoy your delicious homemade lasagna!
