Biryani Recipe

  • Home Page

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 pound chicken, beef, or lamb (cut into pieces)
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 2-3 green chilies, slit
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt and Pepper for taste
  • 1/4 cup chopped mint leaves
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 4 cups water
  • 3-4 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • Whole spices (2-3 cardamom pods, 2-3 cloves, 1-2 bay leaves, and a small piece of cinnamon stick)
  • Fried onions for garnish (optional)
  • Boiled eggs for garnish (optional)
  • Cashews and raisins for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. Wash the basmati rice under cold water until the water runs clear. Soak it in water for 30 minutes, then drain.
  2. In a large pot, heat oil or ghee over medium heat. Add the whole spices and sauté for a minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the sliced onions and cook until golden brown.
  4. Add the ginger-garlic paste and green chilies, and sauté for another minute.
  5. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they soften.
  6. Add the meat pieces and cook until they are browned on all sides.
  7. Stir in the yogurt, turmeric powder, garam masala, salt, and pepper. Cook for 5-7 minutes until the meat is cooked through.
  8. Add the chopped mint and cilantro leaves, and mix well.
  9. Add the soaked and drained rice to the pot, and gently mix it with the meat and spices.
  10. Add 4 cups of water and bring it to a boil. Once boiling, reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and let it simmer for about 20-25 minutes or until the rice is cooked and the water is absorbed.
  11. Once cooked, remove from heat and let it sit covered for 10 minutes.
  12. Fluff the biryani with a fork and garnish with fried onions, boiled eggs, cashews, and raisins if desired.
  13. Serve hot with raita (yogurt sauce) or salad.
  14. Enjoy your delicious homemade biryani!
