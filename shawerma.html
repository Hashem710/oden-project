Shawarma Recipe

  • Home Page

Ingredients

  • 1 pound chicken, beef, or lamb (thinly sliced)
  • 1/4 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Pita bread or flatbreads for serving
  • Tahini sauce or garlic sauce for drizzling
  • Sliced vegetables (tomatoes, cucumbers, onions) for garnish
  • Fresh parsley or cilantro for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, cumin, paprika, turmeric, salt, and pepper.
  2. Add the sliced meat to the marinade and mix well. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (or overnight for best results).
  3. Preheat a grill or skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Cook the marinated meat for about 5-7 minutes on each side until cooked through and slightly charred.
  5. Remove from heat and let it rest for a few minutes before slicing into thin strips.
  6. Warm the pita bread or flatbreads on the grill or in a skillet.
  7. Assemble the shawarma by placing the sliced meat on the bread, drizzling with tahini sauce or garlic sauce, and adding sliced vegetables.
  8. Garnish with fresh parsley or cilantro if desired.
  9. Wrap the shawarma and enjoy!
  10. Serve with extra sauce on the side.
  11. Enjoy your delicious homemade shawarma!
